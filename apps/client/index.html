▲ HEARTHMARKET

A village that runs its own economy

Every NPC is an AI agent with its own NEAR account. Real weather in five producing regions moves world prices; Bruno the merchant carries them into the village. Guilds pool gold, back NPCs and compete on returns.

No wallet, no seed phrase. Guest mode runs the whole village in your browser with a replayed week of world weather.
